# The Revolutionary Review — Issue 1

## Goal
Turn the newsletter into **The Revolutionary Review**, a monthly online PRC publication, and launch a downloadable first issue focused on Kenya from a Trotskyist perspective.

## Website
- Restyle `/newsletter` in the selected **Academic Broadside** direction: paper-and-ink publication masthead, strong rules, serif editorial typography, red PRC accents, issue metadata, and restrained motion.
- Feature Issue 1 prominently with its lead article, contents list, reading links, subscription form, and a clear PDF download.
- Rename newsletter wording in the footer and admin area to “The Revolutionary Review” and change the cadence from weekly to monthly.
- Keep existing future issues and admin publishing compatible with the redesigned archive.

## First issue
Create an inaugural September 2026 issue titled **Kenya at the Crossroads** with original editorial articles covering:
1. Two years after the Gen Z uprising: state repression, accountability, and the need for working-class leadership.
2. Debt and austerity: who pays for Kenya’s fiscal crisis.
3. The nurses’ strike and the struggle for a unified public health service.
4. Femicide, women’s liberation, and the material roots of oppression.
5. The broad-based government and the need for independent working-class politics.
6. East African labour migration and international socialist solidarity.

The articles will clearly separate verified facts from PRC analysis, use careful sourcing, and present a Trotskyist programme rather than unsupported claims.

## PDF edition
- Produce a print-ready A4 PDF matching the Academic Broadside design.
- Include cover, editorial, full articles, issue contents, pull quotes, source notes, and PRC contact details.
- Add it to the site as a downloadable publication.
- Render every PDF page to images and inspect all pages for clipping, overlaps, missing glyphs, poor contrast, and ordering; revise until clean.

## Technical details
- Store the issue content in a reusable typed content module so the web edition and PDF remain aligned.
- Use existing PRC photos where appropriate, with designed typographic spreads where no suitable photograph exists.
- Preserve the current subscriber form and admin permissions.
- Verify the redesigned page on desktop and mobile, confirm the download works, and check the project’s current build status.
