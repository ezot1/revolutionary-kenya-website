# Use authentic article photography

## Goal
Replace generated article illustrations with real photographs of the event, people, or subject discussed wherever a credible reusable image exists.

## Changes
- Audit every generated publication and Revolutionary Review image.
- Prefer authentic PRC-owned uploads for PRC events and original organisational material.
- For public events and historical subjects, use photographs with clear reuse permission from official or Creative Commons sources.
- Keep a realistic generated image only when no legally reusable, truthful photograph can be confirmed.
- Add accurate alternative text and visible source/credit details where the image licence requires attribution.
- Rebuild the Revolutionary Review PDF so it matches the online edition.
- Check the publication pages, newsletter issue, image loading, sharing previews, and PDF download.

## Content safeguards
- Do not use unrelated stock images or present a generic crowd as a specific event.
- Do not copy restricted press photography from news websites.
- Preserve the existing design, article text, and original user-uploaded photographs.
